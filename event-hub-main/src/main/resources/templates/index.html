Why EventHub?

Instant booking

Pick the number of seats and confirm. Your booking appears in your account right away.

Real venues

Every event is linked to a venue with its city, address and capacity, so you know exactly where to go.

Easy management

Organizers create, edit, publish or hide events and keep track of bookings from a dashboard.

Two ways to use the platform

Attendee

  • Browse and search published events
  • Book seats and cancel before the event starts
  • Review everything on the My Bookings page

Organizer

  • Create, edit and delete events
  • Choose a venue for each event
  • Publish or hide events and follow bookings
